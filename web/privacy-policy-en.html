RapidReader - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Introduction

Thank you for using RapidReader. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

Data We Collect

RapidReader keeps the following data:

  • Reading settings: your preferences such as reading speed, font size and chunk size are stored locally on your device.
  • Your texts: the texts you add (typed, imported from a file, pasted from the clipboard, fetched from a web address or received through other apps' Share menu) are stored locally on your device and are not sent to our servers. The clipboard is only read when you tap "Paste".
  • Reading position: where you left off in a book is stored only on your device.

Advertising

Our app uses the Google AdMob advertising service. Google may use device identifiers and cookies to personalize ads. For Google's privacy policy, see: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown Google's consent form when the app opens, and ads are only loaded after that choice. You can change your choice later in the app under Settings > Privacy > Ad consent.

Web Articles

When you open a page with "Web Address" or send a link through the Share menu, the page is downloaded directly from that site. Like with any web request, the site can see technical information such as your IP address, and its own privacy policy applies. The address and the page text are not sent to us.

Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties. Apart from the advertising and web article services described above, no data leaves your device; all reading data and preferences are stored locally on your device.

Data Security

Your data is kept securely in your device's local storage. No personal data is transferred to our servers.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page.
