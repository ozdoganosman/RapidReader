## App name (max 30 characters)

RapidReader: Speed Reading

## Short description (max 80 characters)

Speed read word by word, with guided reading and an easy-on-the-eyes page.

## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Read word by word with RapidReader, keeping your eyes on a single point. The focus letter of every word is marked in red; since your eyes don't have to travel along the line, you read faster and stay focused.

Open every chapter the way you like:

⚡ SPEED READING
• 100 to 1000 words per minute
• Words one at a time or in meaningful groups of 2–3
• Automatically slower for long words and at the end of sentences
• Speed warm-up and gradual speed-up: start slow and climb to your target speed
• Tap the left edge of the screen to go back to the start of the sentence

📄 PLAIN TEXT
• Read the text as a page, at your own pace
• Guided reading: a highlight moves word by word at the speed you choose
• Fonts (Literata, Merriweather, Lora, OpenDyslexic and more) and text size
• Light, sepia, gray, dark and night themes, or your own background and text color
• Brightness control that is easy on the eyes

📚 LIBRARY
• A Tale of Two Cities (Charles Dickens), the original English text
• The Metamorphosis (Franz Kafka), newly translated from the German
• Stories by Ömer Seyfettin, translated from the Turkish
• The Qur'an, the English translation by Marmaduke Pickthall, with the Arabic text
• Your place is saved; get back to it with one tap on "Continue" on the home screen

✍️ YOUR OWN TEXTS
• Type or paste from the clipboard
• Open TXT, PDF and EPUB files
• Fetch the article text from a web address
• Send text from other apps with "Share"

No account needed. Your settings, texts and reading position are stored only on your device.

Privacy policy: https://ozdoganosman.github.io/RapidReader/privacy-policy-en.html
